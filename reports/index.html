Exact two-sided Garwood intervals assume Poisson counts and known exposure. Zero removals have a positive upper bound; MTBUR is undefined at zero removals. September's missing hours block a rate. The screen compares an interval to a baseline point estimate, without repeated-look or baseline-uncertainty adjustment. It is a review aid, not an approved alert limit or maintenance-program change.
